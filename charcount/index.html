How the counts work

Characters count every symbol including spaces and line breaks (emoji and accented letters count as one). "Characters (no spaces)" strips all whitespace. Words are runs of non-space characters, sentences end at . ! ? or …, and paragraphs are blocks separated by a blank line.

Your text never leaves this page — there is no server, no upload, and no tracking. It is remembered only in this browser so it is still here when you come back.
