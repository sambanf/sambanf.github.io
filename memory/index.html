Rules

All cards start face down. Flip any two — if they match, they stay open; if not, they flip back. The timer starts on your first flip, and every two flips count as one move. Find all pairs to win; your best (fewest moves) is saved per difficulty.

Runs entirely in your browser. Best scores are remembered only on this device.
