Saves a single self-contained HTML file with your photo inside. Open it on any device — phone, tablet, laptop — to play with no internet, no app, and nothing uploaded anywhere.

How it works

Tap a tile next to the empty space to slide it in (or use the arrow keys). One tile is always missing — rearrange the rest until your photo is whole again. Bigger grids are harder. "Numbers" shows each tile's home position, and "Peek" reveals the finished picture.

Your photo never leaves your device: it is cropped to a square and resized right here in your browser. The download button bundles the game and your picture into one HTML file you can keep, replay offline, or share by sending the file itself.

Nothing is uploaded and nothing is tracked. Your current puzzle is saved only on this device.
