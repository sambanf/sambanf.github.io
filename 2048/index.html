Rules

Each move slides every tile as far as it can go. Two tiles with the same number merge into one with their sum, and each merge adds to your score. After every move a new 2 (or occasionally a 4) appears. The game ends when the board is full and no merges are possible.

Runs entirely in your browser. Your game and best score are remembered only on this device.
