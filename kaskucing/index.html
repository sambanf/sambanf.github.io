Offline by design

The offline promise

Most money apps want a signal, a login and your finances on their server. Kaskucing doesn't.

Kaskucing does not declare Android's INTERNET permission.

No need to take it on trust: Android blocks network access for any app without that permission. What you earn, spend and have left stays on your phone.

The build checks it too. If any library adds the permission, the release won't build, so it can't reach Google Play.

Works with the quota goneTrain, basement, dead zone: you can still record and check the month.
Nothing to renewNo sign-up, no subscription pop-ups, no server that can go down with your records.
Nothing can leakNo analytics, ads or crash reports. Data that can't leave can't leak.

What it does

Fast to write, honest to read

Most money apps are abandoned in week two. Kaskucing makes each entry take seconds.

Five seconds, three taps

Amount, category, done. The keypad has a calculator, and "save and add another" keeps wallet, category and date.

Every wallet in one book

Cash, bank, e-wallet, credit and paylater, savings. Transfers between them never count as income or expense.

Categories already set up

Makanan, Transportasi, Tagihan and more, ready on first launch. Most-used ones rise to the top; add a missing one mid-entry.

Where the month went

Spending by category, monthly income vs expense and your top five costs. Tap a slice to see its transactions.

One income, four groups

Split income by percentage across Needs, Recurring Needs, Flex Budget and Saving. Get alerts at 80% and 100%.

Rent and subscriptions post themselves

Set a daily, weekly, monthly or yearly rule once. Missed entries land on their own dates, not today.

Backups you own

One file holds everything. Save it anywhere, export CSV for spreadsheets, and restore by replacing or merging.

Yours to set up

Indonesian or English, light or dark, local Rupiah format, any month-start date, and one tap to hide every figure.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Kaskucing has no INTERNET permission, so every feature works in airplane mode.

Is there an account or a subscription?

Neither. No sign-up, no login, no server, and no features behind a paywall.

Does it read bank SMS or link to my accounts?

No, on purpose. Reading bank SMS needs access to every message on the phone, and linking accounts needs the internet. Every figure is one you entered.

Can I keep cash and my e-wallets apart?

Yes. Give each its own wallet and balance: cash, bank, GoPay, OVO, a credit card. Transfers don't count as income or expense.

What if I change phones?

Export a backup from Settings, send it via WhatsApp or a cloud folder, and import it on the new phone.

Can I export my data?

Yes: a full JSON backup, or a CSV of transactions for Excel or Google Sheets. Both save where you choose.

How do I delete everything?

Wipe all data in Settings (you'll confirm twice), or uninstall the app, which permanently removes the database. Delete your own backup files separately.
