Offline by design

The offline promise

Most shop apps need a signal, a login and a subscription. Stokancil doesn't.

Stokancil does not declare Android's INTERNET permission.

No need to take it on trust: Android blocks network access for any app without that permission. Your prices, takings and customers' purchases stay in the shop.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Stokancil has no INTERNET permission, so every feature works in airplane mode.

Is there an account or a subscription?

Neither. No sign-up, no login, no server.

Which printers work with it?

Thermal printers paired in Android's Bluetooth settings, and USB printers. Stokancil never scans for nearby devices.

What if I change phones?

Back up from the app's settings, then restore on the new phone. Android's automatic backup may also move your data, if enabled.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Export your stock as a CSV, or take a full backup. Both save wherever you choose.

How do I delete everything?

Uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android's app settings; either permanently removes the database. Delete your own backup files separately.

Your shop's data belongs in your shop

Stokancil keeps it there. Counter or storeroom, signal or not.
