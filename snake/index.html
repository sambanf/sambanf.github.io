Ready?

Press Space or tap Start. Steer with arrows, WASD, or swipe.

Tip: you can also pause with Space.

Rules

Every food eaten makes the snake one segment longer and adds a point — and every fifth food speeds the game up a little. The run ends if you hit a wall or your own tail. The game pauses automatically if you switch tabs.

Runs entirely in your browser. Your high score is remembered only on this device.
